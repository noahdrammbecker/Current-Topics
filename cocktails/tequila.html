Tequila & Mezcal

Agave and smoke from south of the border. Blue weber and espadín.

Margarita

The queen of tequila cocktails. Salty rim, sour lime, endless summer.

Ingredients

  • Tequila Blanco2 oz
  • Triple Sec1 oz
  • Lime Juice1 oz
  • Saltrim
  • Lime Wheelgarnish

Method

  1. Run a lime wedge around the rim and dip in salt.
  2. Combine tequila, triple sec, and lime in a shaker with ice.
  3. Shake until well-chilled.
  4. Strain into the prepared glass over ice. Garnish with lime.

Paloma

Mexico's favorite. Grapefruit and tequila dance under the desert sun.

Ingredients

  • Tequila Blanco2 oz
  • Grapefruit Soda4 oz
  • Lime Juice1/2 oz
  • Saltpinch
  • Grapefruit Wedgegarnish

Method

  1. Add tequila, lime juice, and a pinch of salt to a highball glass with ice.
  2. Top with grapefruit soda.
  3. Stir gently to combine.
  4. Garnish with a grapefruit wedge.

Prickly Pear Margarita

Desert pink and dangerously smooth. The cactus fruit's sweet revenge.

Ingredients

  • Blanco Tequila2 oz
  • Cointreau1 oz
  • Prickly Pear Syrup¾ oz
  • Lime Juice1 oz
  • Salt Rimoptional

Method

  1. Rim glass with salt if desired.
  2. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  3. Shake until well-chilled.
  4. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.

Oaxaca Old Fashioned

The Old Fashioned goes south. Smoke and agave in perfect harmony.

Ingredients

  • Mezcal1 1/2 oz
  • Tequila Reposado1/2 oz
  • Agave Syrup1 tsp
  • Angostura Bitters2 dashes
  • Orange Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Add agave syrup and bitters to a rocks glass.
  2. Add mezcal, tequila, and a large ice cube.
  3. Stir gently to combine.
  4. Flame an orange peel over the surface and drop in.

Naked and Famous

Equal parts courage. Mezcal meets chartreuse in a modern classic.

Ingredients

  • Mezcal3/4 oz
  • Yellow Chartreuse3/4 oz
  • Aperol3/4 oz
  • Lime Juice3/4 oz

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well-chilled.
  3. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. No garnish—let the smoke speak.

Salt Air Margarita

A cloud of sea salt floats above the glass. Modern alchemy.

Ingredients

  • Blanco Tequila2 oz
  • Cointreau1 oz
  • Lime Juice1 oz
  • Salt Air Foamtopping

Method

  1. Combine tequila, Cointreau, and lime juice in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.
  4. Top with salt air foam.
