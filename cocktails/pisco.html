Pisco & Cachaça

South American fire. From the Andes to Brazil.

Pisco Sour

Peru's pride. Frothy, citrus-forward, and deceptively strong.

Ingredients

  • Pisco 2 oz
  • Lime Juice 1 oz
  • Simple Syrup 3/4 oz
  • Egg White 1
  • Angostura Bitters Garnish

Method

  1. Dry shake all ingredients (without ice) vigorously for 15 seconds.
  2. Add ice and shake again until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  4. Dash bitters atop the foam in a decorative pattern.

Caipirinha

Brazil's national cocktail. Muddled lime, raw sugar, and sugarcane spirit.

Ingredients

  • Cachaça 2 oz
  • Lime 1, quartered
  • Simple Syrup 3/4 oz

Method

  1. Muddle lime quarters with simple syrup in a rocks glass.
  2. Add cachaça and fill with crushed ice.
  3. Stir briefly to combine.
  4. Serve with a short straw.
