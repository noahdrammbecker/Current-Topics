Vodka

Clean. Clear. The ghost in the glass that carries any flavor you give it.

Cosmopolitan

Pink, tart, and unapologetically glamorous. The city's favorite confession.

Ingredients

  • Citrus Vodka (or plain vodka)1 ½ oz
  • Cointreau¾ oz
  • Lime Juice¼ oz
  • Cranberry Juice1 oz
  • Orange Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  4. Flame an orange peel over the surface, express the oils, and drop in.

Espresso Martini

Wake up and wind down, all in one glass. The bartender's nightcap.

Ingredients

  • Vodka2 oz
  • Mr. Black Coffee Liqueur (or Kahlúa)1 oz
  • Fresh Espresso, cooled1 oz
  • Simple Syrup½ oz
  • Coffee Beans3, garnish

Method

  1. Combine vodka, coffee liqueur, cooled espresso, and syrup in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake vigorously for 15 seconds until frothy and well-chilled.
  3. Double strain into a chilled coupe or martini glass.
  4. Float three coffee beans on the foam—for health, wealth, and happiness.

Dirty Martini

For those who like their drinks the way they like their secrets—briny and bold.

Ingredients

  • Vodka2 1/2 oz
  • Dry Vermouth1/2 oz
  • Olive Brine1/2 oz
  • Olives2-3, garnish

Method

  1. Combine vodka, vermouth, and olive brine in a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir until very cold.
  3. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  4. Garnish with olives on a pick.

Moscow Mule

Ginger and lime kick like the name suggests. Best served in copper, cold as Moscow.

Ingredients

  • Vodka2 oz
  • Lime Juice1/2 oz
  • Ginger Beer4-6 oz
  • Lime Wheelgarnish
  • Mint Spriggarnish

Method

  1. Add vodka and lime juice to a copper mug filled with ice.
  2. Top with ginger beer.
  3. Stir gently to combine.
  4. Garnish with a lime wheel and mint sprig.
