Brandy & Cognac

Distilled wine and old-world elegance. Calvados to cognac.

Sidecar

Paris in a glass. Cognac, citrus, and a sugared rim from the Roaring Twenties.

Ingredients

  • Cognac2 oz
  • Cointreau3/4 oz
  • Lemon Juice3/4 oz
  • Sugarrim (optional)
  • Orange Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Optional: rim half the glass with sugar.
  2. Combine cognac, Cointreau, and lemon juice in a shaker with ice.
  3. Shake until well-chilled.
  4. Strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with orange peel.

Corpse Reviver No. 1

The morning-after remedy. Calvados, cognac, and vermouth bring you back.

Ingredients

  • Calvados1 oz
  • Cognac1 oz
  • Sweet Vermouth1 oz

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Serve without garnish—revival needs no decoration.

Brandy Alexander

Dessert in a coupe. Velvety, chocolate-kissed, dangerously drinkable.

Ingredients

  • Cognac1 1/2 oz
  • Dark Crème de Cacao1 oz
  • Heavy Cream1 oz
  • Fresh Nutmeggarnish

Method

  1. Combine cognac, crème de cacao, and cream in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake vigorously until well-chilled and frothy.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Grate fresh nutmeg generously over the top.

Jack Rose

American apple brandy goes pink. Tart, rosy, and utterly charming.

Ingredients

  • Applejack or Calvados2 oz
  • Lime Juice3/4 oz
  • Grenadine1/2 oz
  • Lemon Twistgarnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Garnish with a lemon twist.
