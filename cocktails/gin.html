The Gilded Goose

Our house cocktail. A flight of geese in gold leaf—our creation, our signature.

Ingredients

  • Plymouth Gin2 ½ oz
  • Lemon Juice¾ oz
  • Honey Syrup1 oz
  • Egg White (of exceptional quality)1
  • Angostura Bittersgarnish
  • Gold Leafgarnish

Method

  1. Add all ingredients into a boston shaker with ice and shake.
  2. Pour the contents into a large coupe.
  3. Allow the foam to settle out.
  4. Garnish with flat lines of Angostura bitters in a V pattern.
  5. Run a toothpick through each line to create a V of geese.
  6. Add a small amount of gold leaf to the center, and serve.

Martini

Not shaken, not stirred…

Ingredients

  • Sacred English Dry Vermouth (or Cocci Dry Vermouth)1-3 spritz
  • Plymouth Gin, chilled3 oz
  • Lemon Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Using a small spray bottle, spritz dry vermouth into a chilled martini glass.
  2. Pour gin directly from a bottle that has been stored in the freezer for at least 10 hours.
  3. Cut a large peel of lemon, express the oils over the top of the glass, wiping the oiled side around the rim, and garnish.

Vesper

Bond's creation. Gin, vodka, and quinquina in dangerous proportion.

Ingredients

  • Plymouth Gin3 measures
  • Chopin Vodka1 measure
  • Kina L'Aéro d'Or (or Byrrh, or another quinquina)½ measure
  • Lemon Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Add all three ingredients into a boston shaker with a single medium-sized ice cube.
  2. Shake until chilled.
  3. Pour the contents into a chilled martini glass.
  4. Cut a large peel of lemon, express the oils over the top of the glass, wiping the oiled side around the rim, and garnish.

Bijou

The jewel. Diamond, ruby, and emerald sparkle together.

Ingredients

  • Gin1 ½ oz
  • Sweet Vermouth¾ oz
  • Green Chartreuse¾ oz
  • Orange Bitters2 dashes
  • Luxardo Cherrygarnish

Method

  1. Add all ingredients to a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir for 30-45 seconds until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  4. Garnish with a Luxardo cherry.

Ramos Gin Fizz

New Orleans decadence. Shake until your arms ache.

Ingredients

  • Gin (Old Tom preferred)2 oz
  • Lemon Juice½ oz
  • Lime Juice½ oz
  • Simple Syrup¾ oz
  • Heavy Cream1 ½ oz
  • Egg White1
  • Orange Blossom Water5 drops
  • Club Soda2 oz

Method

  1. Dry shake all ingredients except soda vigorously for 2 minutes.
  2. Add ice and shake for another minute.
  3. Strain into a chilled collins glass (no ice).
  4. Top with club soda, letting the foam rise above the rim.

Bramble

Blackberry and gin. The hedgerow in a glass.

Ingredients

  • Gin1 1/2 oz
  • Lemon Juice3/4 oz
  • Simple Syrup1/2 oz
  • Crème de Mûre1/2 oz
  • Champagne (optional)Splash

Method

  1. Shake gin, lemon, and simple with ice.
  2. Strain over crushed ice in a rocks glass.
  3. Drizzle crème de mûre over the top.
  4. Optionally finish with a splash of champagne.
  5. Garnish with blackberries and a lemon slice.
