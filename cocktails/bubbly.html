Bubbly

Champagne, prosecco, and sparkling dreams.

French 75

Named for the French 75mm field gun. It hits with comparable force.

Ingredients

  • Gin 1 oz
  • Lemon Juice 1/2 oz
  • Simple Syrup 1/2 oz
  • Champagne 3 oz

Method

  1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice.
  2. Strain into a champagne flute.
  3. Top with chilled champagne.
  4. Garnish with a lemon twist.

Aperol Spritz

Italian aperitivo. Bitter, bubbly, and impossibly refreshing.

Ingredients

  • Aperol 2 oz
  • Prosecco 3 oz
  • Club Soda 1 oz
  • Orange Slice Garnish

Method

  1. Fill a wine glass with ice.
  2. Add Aperol and prosecco.
  3. Top with a splash of club soda.
  4. Garnish with an orange slice.

Pimm's Cup

English summer in a glass. Herbaceous and effervescent.

Ingredients

  • Pimm's No. 1 2 oz
  • Lemon Soda 4 oz
  • Cucumber, Strawberry, Mint Garnish

Method

  1. Fill a highball glass with ice.
  2. Add Pimm's and top with lemon soda.
  3. Stir gently.
  4. Garnish lavishly with cucumber, strawberry, and mint.

Kir Royale

Burgundian elegance. Blackcurrant and champagne.

Ingredients

  • Crème de Cassis 1/2 oz
  • Champagne 5 oz

Method

  1. Pour cassis into a champagne flute.
  2. Top slowly with chilled champagne.
  3. Do not stir—let it swirl naturally.

Note: A regular Kir uses white wine instead of champagne.

Death in the Afternoon

Hemingway's creation. Absinthe meets champagne.

Ingredients

  • Absinthe 1 1/2 oz
  • Champagne 4-5 oz

Method

  1. Pour absinthe into a champagne flute.
  2. Slowly top with chilled champagne until opalescent.

Bramble

Gin and blackberry, finished with a fizzy crown.

Ingredients

  • Gin 1 1/2 oz
  • Lemon Juice 3/4 oz
  • Simple Syrup 1/2 oz
  • Crème de Mûre 1/2 oz
  • Champagne Splash (optional)

Method

  1. Shake gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice.
  2. Strain over crushed ice in a rocks glass.
  3. Drizzle crème de mûre over the top.
  4. Optional: finish with a splash of champagne.
  5. Garnish with blackberries and a lemon wheel.
