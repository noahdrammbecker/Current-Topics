Cream Cocktails

Decadent. Indulgent. Dessert in a glass.

Grasshopper

Mint chocolate dreams. A dessert masquerading as a cocktail.

Ingredients

  • Crème de Menthe (Green) 1 oz
  • Crème de Cacao (White) 1 oz
  • Heavy Cream 1 oz

Method

  1. Shake all ingredients vigorously with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  3. Optionally garnish with shaved chocolate or a mint leaf.

Key Lime Martini

Florida sunshine in liquid form. Creamy, tart, and tropical.

Ingredients

  • Vodka 1 1/2 oz
  • Licor 43 1/2 oz
  • Vanilla Liqueur 1/2 oz
  • Lime Juice 3/4 oz
  • Heavy Cream 1 oz

Method

  1. Shake all ingredients vigorously with ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled martini glass.
  3. Garnish with a lime wheel or graham cracker rim.

White Russian

The Dude abides. Coffee, vodka, and cream.

Ingredients

  • Vodka 2 oz
  • Kahlúa 1 oz
  • Heavy Cream 1 oz

Method

  1. Fill a rocks glass with ice.
  2. Add vodka and Kahlúa, stir.
  3. Float cream on top by pouring over the back of a spoon.

Chocolate Flip

A whole egg, chartreuse, and cocoa. Medieval decadence.

Ingredients

  • Yellow Chartreuse 1 oz
  • Maraschino Liqueur 1 oz
  • Dutch Cocoa Powder 1 tsp
  • Whole Egg 1

Method

  1. Dry shake all ingredients vigorously (no ice) for 20 seconds.
  2. Add ice and shake again until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  4. Dust with additional cocoa powder.

Note: Flips traditionally contain a whole egg, giving them an incredibly rich, velvety texture.
