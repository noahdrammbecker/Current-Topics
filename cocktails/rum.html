Caribbean sugar and island dreams. Warm enough to forget the Missouri winter.

Mojito

Havana in a glass. Mint, lime, and summer that never ends.

Ingredients

  • White Rum2 oz
  • Lime Juice1 oz
  • Simple Syrup3/4 oz
  • Fresh Mint8-10 leaves
  • Club Soda2 oz
  • Mint Spriggarnish

Method

  1. Gently muddle mint leaves with simple syrup in a highball glass.
  2. Add rum, lime juice, and fill with ice.
  3. Top with club soda and stir gently.
  4. Garnish with a fresh mint sprig.

Daiquiri

Three ingredients, perfect balance. The drink Hemingway couldn't refuse.

Ingredients

  • White Rum2 oz
  • Lime Juice¾ oz
  • Demerara Syrup (or Simple Syrup)½ oz
  • Lime Wheelgarnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake vigorously for 20 seconds until well-chilled.
  3. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Garnish with a lime wheel.

Mai Tai

Tiki royalty. Orgeat, rum, and paradise, shaken until the islands call.

Ingredients

  • Aged Rum1 1/2 oz
  • White Rum1/2 oz
  • Orange Curaçao1/2 oz
  • Orgeat1/2 oz
  • Lime Juice1 oz
  • Mint Spriggarnish
  • Lime Shellgarnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over crushed ice.
  4. Garnish with spent lime shell and a mint sprig.

Dark 'n' Stormy

Bermuda's official cocktail. Dark rum clouds rolling over ginger waves.

Ingredients

  • Dark Rum2 oz
  • Ginger Beer4-5 oz
  • Lime Juice1/2 oz
  • Lime Wedgegarnish

Method

  1. Fill a highball glass with ice.
  2. Add lime juice and ginger beer.
  3. Float dark rum on top.
  4. Garnish with a lime wedge. Stir if desired.

Piña Colada

Creamy, tropical, unashamed. If you like getting caught in the rain.

Ingredients

  • White Rum2 oz
  • Coconut Cream1 1/2 oz
  • Pineapple Juice2 oz
  • Lime Juice1/2 oz
  • Pineapple Wedgegarnish

Method

  1. Blend all ingredients with a cup of ice until smooth.
  2. Pour into a hurricane glass or tiki mug.
  3. Garnish with a pineapple wedge.
  4. Umbrella optional but encouraged.

Hurricane

New Orleans' liquid storm. Sweet, strong, and devastating.

Ingredients

  • Dark Rum2 oz
  • White Rum1 oz
  • Passion Fruit Juice2 oz
  • Orange Juice1 oz
  • Lime Juice1/2 oz
  • Grenadine1/2 oz
  • Orange Slicegarnish
  • Cherrygarnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a hurricane glass over ice.
  4. Garnish with an orange slice and cherry.

Jungle Bird

Bitter meets tropical. A tiki drink with a Campari edge.

Ingredients

  • Dark Rum1 1/2 oz
  • Campari3/4 oz
  • Pineapple Juice1 1/2 oz
  • Lime Juice1/2 oz
  • Simple Syrup1/2 oz
  • Pineapple Leafgarnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.
  4. Garnish with a pineapple leaf.

Painkiller

Exactly what it sounds like. Creamy, coconut, and strong enough to help.

Ingredients

  • Aged Rum2 oz
  • Pineapple Juice4 oz
  • Orange Juice1 oz
  • Coconut Cream1 oz
  • Fresh Nutmeggarnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake vigorously until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a tiki mug or highball filled with ice.
  4. Grate fresh nutmeg generously over the top.

El Presidente

Cuba's answer to the Manhattan. Vermouth, curaçao, and revolutionary spirit.

Ingredients

  • White Rum1 1/2 oz
  • Dry Vermouth3/4 oz
  • Orange Curaçao1/2 oz
  • Grenadine1 tsp
  • Orange Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Garnish with an expressed orange peel.

Rum Old Fashioned

The classic, Caribbean-style. Aged rum brings its own sweetness.

Ingredients

  • Aged Rum2 oz
  • Demerara Syrup1/4 oz
  • Angostura Bitters2 dashes
  • Orange Bitters1 dash
  • Orange Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Add syrup and bitters to a rocks glass.
  2. Add rum and a large ice cube.
  3. Stir gently to combine.
  4. Express orange peel over the glass and drop in.
