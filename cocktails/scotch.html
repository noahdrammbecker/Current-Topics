Scotch

Peat and smoke from across the Atlantic. For those who prefer their mysteries aged.

Rob Roy

Scotland's answer to the Manhattan. Named for an outlaw, fit for royalty.

Ingredients

  • Blended Scotch2 oz
  • Sweet Vermouth1 oz
  • Angostura Bitters2 dashes
  • Luxardo Cherrygarnish

Method

  1. Combine scotch, vermouth, and bitters in a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Garnish with a Luxardo cherry.

Penicillin

Modern medicine in a glass. Ginger, honey, and a smoky Islay finish.

Ingredients

  • Blended Scotch2 oz
  • Lemon Juice3/4 oz
  • Honey-Ginger Syrup3/4 oz
  • Islay Scotch1/4 oz float
  • Candied Gingergarnish

Method

  1. Combine blended scotch, lemon juice, and syrup in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.
  4. Float Islay scotch on top. Garnish with candied ginger.

Blood and Sand

Named for a silent film. Cherry, orange, and scotch in equal measure.

Ingredients

  • Blended Scotch3/4 oz
  • Sweet Vermouth3/4 oz
  • Cherry Heering3/4 oz
  • Orange Juice3/4 oz
  • Orange Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well-chilled.
  3. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Garnish with an expressed orange peel.

Old Sport

Scotch and brandy, united. No garnish needed—ambition speaks for itself.

Ingredients

  • Scotch1 ½ oz
  • Brandy1 oz
  • Sweet Vermouth½ oz
  • Bruto Americano½ oz

Method

  1. Add ingredients into a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir until chilled.
  3. Pour into a rocks glass with a single large cube.
  4. Serve ungarnished.
