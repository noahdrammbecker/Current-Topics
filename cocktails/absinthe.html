Absinthe

The green fairy. Handle with reverence.

Death in the Afternoon

Hemingway's creation. Absinthe meets champagne in a dangerous liaison.

Ingredients

  • Absinthe 1 1/2 oz
  • Champagne 4-5 oz

Method

  1. Pour absinthe into a champagne flute.
  2. Slowly top with chilled champagne until it reaches opalescence.
  3. No garnish. No ice. No mercy.

Hemingway's Note: "Drink three to five of these slowly."

Sazerac

New Orleans royalty. Rye, bitters, and an absinthe rinse.

Ingredients

  • Rye Whiskey 2 oz
  • Peychaud's Bitters 3 dashes
  • Simple Syrup 1/4 oz
  • Absinthe Rinse
  • Lemon Peel Garnish

Method

  1. Rinse a chilled rocks glass with absinthe; discard excess.
  2. In a mixing glass, combine rye, bitters, and simple syrup with ice.
  3. Stir until well-chilled.
  4. Strain into the prepared glass (no ice).
  5. Express lemon peel over the drink and discard.

Corpse Reviver No. 2

The morning-after cure. Equal parts everything, plus a whisper of absinthe.

Ingredients

  • Gin 3/4 oz
  • Lillet Blanc 3/4 oz
  • Cointreau 3/4 oz
  • Lemon Juice 3/4 oz
  • Absinthe Rinse

Method

  1. Rinse a chilled coupe with absinthe; discard excess.
  2. Shake remaining ingredients with ice.
  3. Strain into the prepared glass.
  4. Garnish with a cherry if desired.

Warning: "Four of these taken in swift succession will unrevive the corpse again."

Necromancer

A Corpse Reviver's darker cousin. Elderflower replaces orange.

Ingredients

  • Gin 1/2 oz
  • Lillet Blanc 3/4 oz
  • Elderflower Liqueur 3/4 oz
  • Lemon Juice 3/4 oz
  • Absinthe Rinse

Method

  1. Rinse a chilled coupe with absinthe; discard excess.
  2. Shake remaining ingredients with ice.
  3. Strain into the prepared glass.

Millionaire

Bourbon meets absinthe. For those who've already made their fortune.

Ingredients

  • Bourbon 1 1/2 oz
  • Grand Marnier 1/2 oz
  • Lemon Juice 3/4 oz
  • Grenadine 1/4 oz
  • Egg White 1
  • Absinthe Rinse

Method

  1. Rinse a chilled coupe with absinthe; discard excess.
  2. Dry shake all other ingredients vigorously.
  3. Add ice and shake again until well-chilled.
  4. Strain into the prepared glass.
