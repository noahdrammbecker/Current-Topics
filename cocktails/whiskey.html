Whiskey & Bourbon

American oak and amber fire. Rye, bourbon, and everything that burns just right.

Manhattan

The drink that built the city. Sweet, strong, and impossible to forget.

Ingredients

  • Rye Whiskey2 oz
  • Sweet Vermouth1 oz
  • Angostura Bitters2 dashes
  • Luxardo Cherrygarnish

Method

  1. Add rye, vermouth, and bitters to a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir until well-chilled, about 30 seconds.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe glass.
  4. Garnish with a Luxardo cherry.

Old Fashioned

The original cocktail. Nothing to hide behind—just spirit, sugar, and time.

Ingredients

  • Bourbon or Rye2 oz
  • Sugar Cube (or ½ oz Simple Syrup)1
  • Angostura Bitters3 dashes
  • Watersplash
  • Orange Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Place sugar cube in a rocks glass. Saturate with bitters and a splash of water.
  2. Muddle until the sugar is nearly dissolved.
  3. Add whiskey and a large ice cube.
  4. Stir gently for 30 seconds until chilled and diluted.
  5. Express orange peel over the glass, rub the rim, and drop in.

Boulevardier

A Negroni's American cousin. All the bitterness, with a whiskey backbone.

Ingredients

  • Rye Whiskey1 1/2 oz
  • Sweet Vermouth1 oz
  • Bruto Americano1 oz
  • Orange Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube.
  4. Garnish with an expressed orange peel.

Revolver

Coffee and rye, drawn at dawn. Named for how quickly it goes down.

Ingredients

  • Rye Whiskey2 oz
  • Mr. Black Coffee Liqueur1/2 oz
  • Orange Bitters2 dashes
  • Orange Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Combine rye, coffee liqueur, and bitters in a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Flame an orange peel over the surface and drop in.

Amaretto Sour

Don't let the almond fool you. This one has teeth.

Ingredients

  • Bourbon1 1/2 oz
  • Amaretto Liqueur3/4 oz
  • Lemon Juice1 oz
  • Simple Syrup1/4 oz
  • Egg White1
  • Luxardo Cherrygarnish

Method

  1. Dry shake all ingredients vigorously without ice.
  2. Add ice and shake again until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a rocks glass over ice.
  4. Garnish with a Luxardo cherry.

Whiskey Sour

Sour, sweet, and silky. The drink that proves simplicity is king.

Ingredients

  • Bourbon or Rye2 oz
  • Lemon Juice3/4 oz
  • Simple Syrup3/4 oz
  • Egg White1
  • Angostura Bittersgarnish

Method

  1. Dry shake whiskey, lemon, syrup, and egg white vigorously.
  2. Add ice and shake again until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe or rocks glass.
  4. Dash bitters on the foam and drag through for design.

Autumn Old Fashioned

When the leaves turn, so does the cocktail. Maple and amaretto warm the bones.

Ingredients

  • Bourbon2 oz
  • Mt. Defiance Amaretto1/2 oz
  • Maple Syrup1/4 oz
  • Trinity Bitters2 dashes
  • Orange Peelgarnish

Method

  1. Add maple syrup and bitters to a rocks glass.
  2. Add bourbon and amaretto with a large ice cube.
  3. Stir gently to combine.
  4. Express orange peel over the glass and drop in.

Paper Plane

Equal parts, perfect balance. Modern classic that landed in 2007 and never left.

Ingredients

  • Bourbon3/4 oz
  • Aperol3/4 oz
  • Amaro Nonino3/4 oz
  • Lemon Juice3/4 oz

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well-chilled.
  3. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. No garnish—let it fly clean.

Flying Fox

Bourbon takes flight with bitter orange. Stirred, not shaken—this one knows what it is.

Ingredients

  • Bourbon1 ½ oz
  • Aperol1 ½ oz
  • Lemon Juice½ oz
  • Orange Bitters4 dashes
  • Dried Orange Wheelgarnish

Method

  1. Add ingredients into a mixing glass with ice.
  2. Stir until chilled.
  3. Pour into a rocks glass with a single large cube.
  4. Garnish with a dried orange wheel.

Scofflaw

Named for those who mocked Prohibition. Defiant, sweet, and wonderfully illegal.

Ingredients

  • Rye Whiskey1 1/2 oz
  • Dry Vermouth1 oz
  • Lemon Juice3/4 oz
  • Grenadine1/2 oz
  • Orange Bitters1 dash
  • Lemon Twistgarnish

Method

  1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker with ice.
  2. Shake until well-chilled.
  3. Strain into a chilled coupe.
  4. Garnish with a lemon twist.

Mint Julep

Southern hospitality in a silver cup. The Derby's liquid crown.

Ingredients

  • Bourbon2 1/2 oz
  • Simple Syrup1/2 oz
  • Fresh Mint8-10 leaves
  • Crushed Iceplenty
  • Mint Spriggarnish

Method

  1. Gently muddle mint leaves with simple syrup in a julep cup.
  2. Add bourbon and pack with crushed ice.
  3. Stir until the cup frosts.
  4. Top with more crushed ice and a generous mint bouquet.
