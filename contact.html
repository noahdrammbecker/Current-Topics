The Location

Balducci's Barbers
(Ask for a sharp shave)

The Riverfront
The Districts
Dael City, Nawshag County, Missouri

Instructions for Entry

  1. Enter through the barbershop. Teddy will be at the front. He talks too much—just nod along.
  2. Proceed to the back. There is a mirror in a tarnished silver frame.
  3. Look at your reflection. If you see only yourself, turn around and leave. The Goose is not for you tonight.
  4. If you see... something else... press the third brass button on the frame's right edge.
  5. The door will ask you a question. Answer correctly.
  6. Welcome to The Gilded Goose.

Hours of Operation

The Gilded Goose opens when it opens
and closes when the last patron forgets to leave.

Generally speaking: dusk until the small hours.
Closed Sundays. Even sinners need rest.

Correspondence

We do not have a telephone.
We do not accept telegrams.
We do not respond to letters sent to this address.

If you need to reach Freddie, you'll find a way.
You always do.

A word of caution: Do not mention The Gilded Goose to the authorities. Do not bring the authorities. Do not be the authorities. Freddie has arrangements, but arrangements have limits. And Mason has a long memory.

"If you can find us, you belong here.
If you cannot, perhaps that is for the best."
— Freddie
