Events

The calendar of The Gilded Goose. Attendance by invitation—or audacity.

Feb 14 1932

A Valentine's Masquerade

9 PM until the masks come off

Cupid shoots blindfolded. So do we. Join us for an evening of mystery, champagne cocktails, and carefully maintained anonymity. Identities optional. Masks mandatory. The jazz will be hot. The gin will be cold. What happens behind the velvet curtain stays there.

Attire: Formal. Faces covered. Hearts optional.

Mar 5 1932

The Bootlegger's Ball

10 PM — ?

A celebration of American ingenuity. We toast to the runners, the smugglers, the men with false-bottomed boats and the women who distracted the agents. Live music from the Mississippi Delta. Dancing until your feet surrender. Freddie will personally pour the first round.

Attire: Your finest. Suspenders encouraged.

Mar 21 1932

Equinox Evening

Sunset to Sunrise

When day and night stand in perfect balance, so too shall we. A twelve-hour affair marking the spring equinox. New cocktails debut at midnight. Mason and Wilson have been preparing something... unusual. The Fabergé egg will be unveiled for viewing. Questions about it will not be answered.

Attire: Ethereal. Think moonlight and mischief.

Apr 16 1932

The Gatsby Affair

8 PM onwards

Old sport, the green light beckons. An evening of decadent excess in the style of East Egg—if East Egg had better gin and fewer moral qualms. Champagne towers. A live orchestra. Dancing on tables is neither encouraged nor prohibited. Come for the party. Stay because you've forgotten why you came.

Attire: Roaring. Absolutely roaring.

May 1 1932

May Day Midnight

11:59 PM

The old traditions hold power still. Flowers in your hair. Fire in your glass. Wilson has sourced herbs from somewhere he won't name, and the cocktail menu for the evening will feature... botanicals of interest. Dance around the maypole we've erected in the back room. Try not to get tangled in the ribbons. Or do. We don't judge.

Attire: Pastoral with a hint of pagan.
